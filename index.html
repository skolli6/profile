About Me

17 Years Building What Moves the World

"I architect embedded systems that run reliably at the edge of the physical world — from plant-floor PLCs to satellite-connected vehicles to smart cities."

I'm Srihari Kolli, an Embedded Systems & IoT Architect with 17+ years of experience delivering mission-critical software for automotive, industrial, and defense sectors. My technical expertise spans C, C++, Python, RTOS, multithreading, MQTT, OPC protocols, and cloud integration with AWS, Azure, and GCP — covering the full stack from bare-metal firmware to cloud-connected dashboards.

Throughout my career I have taken ownership of complete product lifecycles — from architecture and development to deployment and support — ensuring high-quality deliverables and system reliability. Currently building smart energy meters (NexRev) and an AI-driven school security system (Lockout) using PSoC 4-BLE, Azure IoT, and GCP. Previously contributed to GM's Ultifi SDV platform, Ford's SYNC4 infotainment, Hyundai's IIoT Fleet Management (ESP32S3 + FreeRTOS + AWS IoT), and FCA's Factory Information System across North American plants.

What drives me is the opportunity to build intelligent, scalable, cloud-connected systems that enable real-time monitoring, automation, and data-driven decision-making. I'm also a founder — QNow (joinqnow.vercel.app) is my live SaaS product for virtual queue & appointment management, built end-to-end with React, Firebase, and WhatsApp Cloud API.

Early in my career, I developed missile launch applications for BRAHMOS on Lynx RTOS at ECIL/DRDL — an experience that instilled a deep respect for safety-critical, zero-defect software engineering. Open to Senior IoT Platform, Embedded & Firmware Engineering roles in USA, UAE, India & globally.
